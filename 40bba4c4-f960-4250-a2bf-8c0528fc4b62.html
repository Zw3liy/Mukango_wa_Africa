About The Art

A Legacy Carved in Wood

Founded by master craftsman Chiwama Kennedy Daka, Mukango Wa Africa transforms reclaimed, sustainably-sourced timber into heirloom furniture pieces. Based in Lusaka, Zambia, the studio fuses age-old carving traditions with a contemporary design language.

Each collection is a love letter to the African wilderness — the Big Five, the sweeping savannah, the rhythm of village life. Every line and contour is intentional, every finish hand-polished to perfection.

Our pieces are not merely furniture. They are sculptural statements, designed to anchor modern living spaces with stories of heritage and hope.
